# Salon Khata Vision

Salon Khata is a mobile-first digital notebook for small salons, barbershops, and beauty parlours. It replaces the daily paper register used to record income, expenses, employee commission, and basic business totals.

Salon Khata is not a salon ERP. It should feel closer to a fast consumer payments app than accounting software.

## Product Promise

The owner can record daily salon activity in seconds, even without internet, without learning accounting language.

## Target Users

- Small salon owners
- Barbershops
- Beauty parlours
- Businesses with 1 to 10 employees
- Owners who are comfortable with mobile payments but not with complex software

## MVP Scope

The MVP includes:

- Dashboard
- Services
- Employees
- Employee commission rules
- Income entry
- Expense entry
- Reports
- Settings
- Mobile number and OTP authentication
- Offline-first local data storage
- Background cloud synchronization
- Backup and restore
- Internationalization foundation

## Non-Goals

The MVP must not include:

- Customer management
- Appointment booking
- Loyalty program
- Membership
- Product inventory
- GST
- Billing printer
- WhatsApp marketing
- Online booking
- Multi-branch support
- Salary management
- Attendance

These features are intentionally deferred so the first product remains fast, simple, and trustworthy.

## Success Criteria

- A new user understands the app without training.
- Daily income entry can be completed in under 10 seconds.
- Expense entry can be completed in under 10 seconds.
- Core app usage works with no internet connection.
- Reports remain available offline.
- The app feels premium, calm, and approachable on low-cost Android devices.
- No visible string is hardcoded in implementation.

## Product Positioning

Salon Khata should be described as:

> A simple daily khata for salons that works offline and keeps income, expenses, and staff commission clear.

It should not be described as:

> Salon management ERP, billing system, appointment platform, CRM, or accounting suite.
